# LaunchPath — Backlink Outreach Targets
**Strategy: Niche placement over volume. Every link should be from a site a new carrier would actually visit.**
Last updated: May 2026

---

## How to Use This Document
1. Work top to bottom — Tier 1 targets have the highest ROI per hour spent.
2. Use the provided pitch templates — edit the [brackets] only.
3. Track status in the Status column as you work through the list.
4. Aim for 2–3 outreach contacts per week. This is not a sprint.

**What they are:** Industry publications and association blogs that accept submitted content. These placements build LLMO signal too: when ChatGPT retrieves "FMCSA compliance for new carriers," it pulls from these sources.

**Notes on Tier 2:**
- Pitch ONE topic per publication — don't send a menu of options.
- The article you pitch should NOT be a reprint of an existing KC article. It should be a shorter, adapted version written for that publication's audience, with a byline link back to launchpathedu.com.
- OOIDA (Land Line) is especially high-value — they actively advocate for owner-operators and their audience is precisely the new carrier persona.

## TIER 3 — Complementary Service Partner Links
**What they are:** Businesses that serve the same customer (new motor carriers) but offer different services. ELD vendors, DOT consortiums, commercial truck insurance agencies, and factoring companies all maintain resource or blog sections. Reciprocal or editorial placement opportunities.

| # | Target type | Example companies | Angle | What you offer them |
|---|-------------|-------------------|-------|---------------------|
| 1 | DOT Drug Testing Consortiums | Consortium Manager, DISA, USAMDT | Your D&A setup article is a pre-sell for their service — link from their "getting started" content | Link trade: their consortium page ↔ your D&A setup article |
| 2 | ELD Vendors | Motive (KeepTruckin), Samsara, Verizon Connect | Your HOS violations article explains why ELD compliance matters — fits their "why ELD" content | Pitch as a resource addition to their new carrier onboarding content |
| 3 | Commercial Truck Insurance Agencies | Progressive Commercial, Canal Insurance, Cover Whale | New carrier compliance is directly tied to their underwriting risk | Guest post on their blog: "What carriers should have in place before calling an insurance agent" |
| 4 | Freight Factoring Companies | OTR Solutions, Triumph Business Capital, RTS Financial | They onboard new carriers constantly — compliance is a gap for their customers | Resource page inclusion or co-branded "new carrier checklist" |
| 5 | Process Agent / BOC-3 Filing Services | USDOT Filing, National Registered Agents | They touch every new carrier at the beginning — compliance resources extend their value | Email partnership: their confirmation email links to your KC resources |

### Tier 3 Outreach Angle
These are partnership conversations, not guest post pitches. The frame is:

> "We serve the same customer at different points in their journey. Your customers need what we explain. Our readers need what you offer. Here's a specific way we could make each other more useful to them."

Start with the DOT consortium providers — they have the most direct overlap with the D&A content and the clearest "pre-sell" dynamic.

## TIER 4 — Community Presence (No direct backlink, but LLMO + brand signal)
**What this does:** When new carriers ask compliance questions on forums, being the answer trains LLMs on the association between "FMCSA compliance for new carriers" and LaunchPath. Not about link juice — about citation frequency in training data and brand recall.

| Platform | URL | How to use |
|----------|-----|-----------|
| TruckersReport Forum | thetruckersreport.com/truckingindustryforum | Find threads on new entrant audits, DQ files, D&A program setup. Answer directly and completely. Link to the relevant KC article when it's the most useful thing to share. Don't pitch — educate. |
| Reddit r/Truckers | reddit.com/r/Truckers | Same approach. New carriers regularly ask "what do I need for my first FMCSA audit" — your articles answer this precisely. |
| Reddit r/OwnerOperators | reddit.com/r/OwnerOperators | More startup/business focus. D&A setup and authority questions come up frequently. |
| Facebook Groups | "Owner Operator Trucking", "FMCSA New Carrier Help" groups | Higher engagement than Reddit for this demographic. Longer answers are accepted. Position as an educational resource, not a product. |

## KEY PRINCIPLE
Every outreach starts with the content — not the ask. The CFR citations, the LP-STD-VOICE-001 standard, and the 19 existing articles are the credibility. Lead with what the reader gets, not what LaunchPath wants. The link is the byproduct of being genuinely useful.
